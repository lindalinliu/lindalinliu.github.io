About

I am a Professor at the School of Software, Tsinghua University. My research is in requirements engineering, services engineering, and domain knowledge engineering — how software systems, and the data and knowledge they depend on, can be modelled and systematically analysed before, during, and after they are built.

A long-running thread of my work asks how intent — the goals, obligations and social dependencies among the people and organisations a system serves — can be represented precisely enough to be reasoned about. That question runs from goal- and agent-oriented modelling with i* and Tropos, through formal verification of early requirements in Formal Tropos, to security and privacy requirements analysis within a social setting, and more recently to requirements for machine-learning and large-language-model agent systems.

A second thread is applied: turning domain data and expert knowledge into services that people actually rely on — oral health and clinical decision support, industrial safety analytics, and requirements engineering for well-being, ageing and health.

Before joining Tsinghua in 2004, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Toronto, in the Faculty of Information Studies and later in the Department of Computer Science. I hold a Ph.D. from the Institute of Mathematics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and B.S. and M.S. degrees in computer science from Jilin University.

Research

Goal- and agent-oriented modelling

Modelling the strategic actors around a system — their goals, dependencies and vulnerabilities — with i* and Tropos, and using those models to drive design and architectural choices.

Formal analysis of early requirements

Formal Tropos: giving intentional requirements models a temporal-logic semantics so that early specifications can be animated and model-checked before any code exists.

Security & privacy requirements

Analysing security as a relationship among strategic actors rather than a property of a mechanism — Secure-i*, attacker-viewpoint analysis, and security requirements education.

Crowd-based & data-driven RE

Eliciting requirements from what users do and say at scale: behavioural data, online forums, argumentation mining, and the evolution of services in response to changing needs.

Health, well-being and safety

Requirements engineering for well-being, ageing and health; oral health ontologies and big-data services; clinical decision support; industrial safety analytics and accident prevention.

Requirements for AI and agent systems

What "requirements" mean when the artefact is a learned model or an autonomous LLM agent — specification, monitoring, reliability, and accountability at the level of intent.

Teaching & students

I teach and design courses in software engineering at the School of Software, with a particular interest in outcome-based education (OBE) and in what software engineering curricula should look like now that AI assistance is part of ordinary practice — from requirements and design through to full-stack project work, testing and code review.

I supervise M.Sc. and Ph.D. students working on requirements engineering, knowledge graphs and domain ontologies, health and safety informatics, and the specification and reliability of LLM-based agent systems. Prospective students with a background in software engineering, information systems or applied machine learning are welcome to write to me with a CV and a short statement of interest.
